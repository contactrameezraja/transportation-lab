Lab 2 · Lectures 24 and 27

Dual variables as shadow prices

Supply and demand must stay balanced, so the only allowed change adds k units at one supply point and k at one demand point. The optimal dual solution predicts a cost change of k(ui + vj), and the prediction holds while the optimal basis stays feasible (Lecture 27). Re-solving checks it.

Lab 3 · Section 4 of the report

Sinkhorn and soft complementary slackness

The entropic plan is Pij = exp((ui + vj − cij)/ε), so every route carries mass that shrinks with its reduced cost. Circles show amounts. Colour shows the reduced cost at the exact optimum, green for zero (a basic route) and darker red for larger positive values.
